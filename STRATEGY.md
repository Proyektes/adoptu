# Adopt-U — Sustainability & Growth Strategy (nonprofit)

*Research compiled 2026-08-01 (deep-research pass with adversarial claim verification + targeted follow-up). ⚠ = approximate/unverified; everything else has a cited source. Legal items are research, not legal advice.*

## 1. Principle

Free for adopters and rescuers, always — volume is the mission. Sustainability comes from grants/free-ads, donations at goodwill peaks, and sponsor/affiliate revenue that never gates the core.

## 4. The product wedge

Rescuers' entire tooling today = Facebook posts + WhatsApp screening (directories list rescuers with only FB/IG handles). Existing MX platforms (Refugios.mx, HogarMestizo, Refugia.mx, Adoptare, Purina Adopta, Pedigree Adóptame) are **listing boards only — none offer shelter-side management** (intake records, medical history, applicant pipeline). That management layer + lost-pet matching is the open niche — the Miwuki playbook: free shelter tooling as the supply-side acquisition hook.

## 5. Legal path: A.C. → CLUNI → Google Ad Grants (donataria later)

1. **Constitute the A.C.** — notary + registry ≈ **MXN 10,000–25,000**; draft the objeto social to echo LFFAROSC **Art. 5 fracción XII** ("protección del ambiente, la flora y la fauna") — animal-welfare A.C.s qualify under this fraction. — [ley text](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf_mov/Ley_Federal_de_Fomento_a_las_Actividades-por_Organizaciones_de_la_Sociedad_Civil.pdf)
2. **CLUNI** — free, 100% online at corresponsabilidad.gob.mx with e.firma; registry now under **Secretaría de Bienestar** (Indesol extinct since 2021 — ignore older guides); resolution ≤30 business days. Realistic end-to-end from zero: **~2–4 months**. — [gob.mx requisitos](https://www.gob.mx/indesol/acciones-y-programas/requisitos-para-el-tramite-de-inscripcion-al-registro-federal-de-las-osc-49693)
3. **Google for Nonprofits → Ad Grants ($10,000 USD/month in free search ads)** — in Mexico, CLUNI qualifies; donataria NOT required. Goodstack verification ~3–5 business days. This is the single biggest free-traffic lever. — [Google MX eligibility](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/3215869?hl=es-419&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DMX)
4. **Donataria autorizada (SAT), later** — free, ≤3 months legal max, filed online with e.firma, but: requires ≥1 year of operations, and ⚠ **SAT's 12 authorized categories do not explicitly include companion-animal adoption** (the "endangered species" category covers only protected species with SEMARNAT opinion; "ecological activities" as a fit was refuted in verification). Draft with a Mexican fiscal advisor (asistencial/educativa framing). — [SAT ficha 15/ISR](https://www.sat.gob.mx/tramites/71215/solicita-la-autorizacion-para-recibir-donativos-deducibles-del-impuesto-sobre-la-renta), [SAT categories](https://www.gob.mx/sat/acciones-y-programas/actividades-que-pueden-ser-autorizadas-para-recibir-donativos-deducibles)

## 6. Revenue without taint

1. **Ad Grants traffic** → adopters + donors (the "ads" line for this project is free by design).
2. **Recurring donations** ("apadrina un rescate, $50 MXN/mes") + one-off ask at the moment of adoption (goodwill peak).
3. **Sponsors/affiliates** (this is Adopt-U's version of "selling ads to marketers"): pet food brands, veterinarias, pet insurance in front of brand-new owners; sponsored "kit del nuevo adoptante" (Pedigree/Purina both run adoption CSR programs — natural sponsors). Constraint: the AGATAN one-pager promises **no ads on the institutional catalog** — keep sponsor placements on Adopt-U's own surfaces (post-adoption flow, newsletter, kit), never on government partners' listings.
4. **Optional paid services from existing roles**: photographers, sterilization services — small booking fee, never touching the free adoption core.

## 7. International expansion (later — legal status is per-country)

i18n makes the product portable, but the nonprofit machinery is not: A.C./CLUNI/donataria are Mexican constructs, and Google for Nonprofits eligibility is verified per country (each country has its own qualifying registrations). Practical path:

- **Prove the model in Mexico first** — the Miwuki-style free shelter tooling is the portable asset; shelters everywhere run on Facebook + spreadsheets.
- **Spain** is the natural second market (same language, strong adoption culture, Miwuki's home turf proves demand — and also proves competition; differentiate on the shelter-management layer).
- **Per-country entry checklist**: local nonprofit registration (or partner with an existing local NGO instead of incorporating — often faster), Google for Nonprofits eligibility for that country, and the local equivalents of AGATAN/protectoras networks.
- Partnering with established local rescue federations beats incorporating in each country — Adopt-U can be the platform, the local NGO the legal face.
